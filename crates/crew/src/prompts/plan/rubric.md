Score each plan from 0 to 10 on every criterion below, in this order. A higher score is always better. Give evidence from the plan for every score.

- `correctness_risk`: 10 means the lowest risk that the plan is wrong, breaks something, or misses the goal.
- `completeness`: 10 means it covers every success criterion and every piece of the work.
- `testability`: 10 means every part can be checked by tests the crew can write.
- `simplicity`: 10 means nothing in it is more complex than the goal needs.
- `cost`: 10 means the cheapest to build and to run.
